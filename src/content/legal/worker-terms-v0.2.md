## 1. Who these Terms apply to

These Terms apply when you use DREVORA as a Worker, driver, employee, agency worker, contractor or other authorised user of an organisation (your **Organisation**).

Your Organisation gives you access and controls your role, records and permissions. Your Organisation is normally the controller of your employment, workforce, vehicle and operational data. DREVORA normally processes that information for your Organisation.

These Terms are between you and DREVORA for permitted use of the application. They do not make DREVORA your employer and do not change your contract with your Organisation.

## 2. Use only for authorised work

You may use DREVORA only for the Organisation that invited you and only for authorised work purposes.

You must not access another organisation's information, share your account, impersonate another person or help anyone bypass permissions.

## 3. Account and device security

Keep your login details and device secure. Do not share passwords, one-time codes or unlocked access.

Tell your Office and DREVORA support promptly if you think your account or device has been used without permission.

Biometric app lock, where enabled, is handled by your device. DREVORA does not receive your fingerprint, face scan or biometric template.

## 4. Accurate records

You must provide complete and accurate information to the best of your knowledge.

Do not knowingly falsify:

- Timesheets, hours, breaks or confirmations;
- Vehicle Checks or Tyre Checks;
- defects, photographs, signatures or odometer readings;
- Driver Reports, CMR, POD, receipts or documents;
- holiday or other work-related requests.

Your Organisation may review, approve, reject, correct, retain or investigate records under its policies and Applicable Law.

## 5. Vehicle and Tyre Checks

DREVORA is a recording tool. It does not decide that a vehicle is roadworthy and does not replace the legal walkaround-check process, defect escalation or your professional judgement.

Report defects accurately and follow your Organisation's safety procedure. Do not drive or continue to use a vehicle where doing so would be unsafe or unlawful.

A completed check may become read-only to preserve the record. Corrections may be recorded separately rather than overwriting the original submission.

## 6. Do not use DREVORA while driving

Do not read, type, photograph, sign or interact with DREVORA while driving or while using the device would be unsafe or unlawful.

Stop in a safe and lawful place before using the application.

## 7. Timesheets and holiday information

DREVORA may calculate or display hours, overtime, holiday balances and statuses according to settings chosen by your Organisation.

You must review your Timesheet before confirming or submitting it. A calculation or display does not replace payroll review or your Organisation's legal responsibilities.

Questions about pay, entitlement, approval or rejection should normally be directed to your Office.

## 8. Offline use and synchronisation

Some features may work offline and synchronise later.

Offline records may remain on your device until upload finishes. Reconnect when reasonably possible and check whether the record shows as synchronised.

Do not assume that a record has reached your Office until the application confirms completion or synchronisation.

Device loss, browser clearing, app removal, insufficient storage or operating-system cleanup may remove unsynchronised local information. Report unresolved sync problems promptly.

## 9. Photographs, signatures and documents

Upload only information that is relevant to your work and that you are authorised to submit.

Do not upload unrelated personal information, unlawful content, confidential information belonging to another organisation, malware or documents you have no right to use.

Do not photograph people unnecessarily. Follow your Organisation's rules for customer sites, documents, cargo and personal information.

## 10. Privacy

The DREVORA Privacy Policy explains when DREVORA acts as controller and when it acts as processor.

Your Organisation should provide its own Worker Privacy Notice explaining why it uses your workforce and operational information, the lawful basis, access, retention and your rights.

For questions about data entered by your Organisation, contact your Office first. For DREVORA-controlled support, security or account information, contact admin@drevora.uk.

## 11. Support

Use **Contact Office** for work instructions, pay, holiday, approvals, vehicle decisions and Organisation policies.

Use **DREVORA Support** for application bugs, access problems, technical issues and product feedback.

Support information may include limited technical metadata such as app version, platform, screen, network state and time. It must not include your password or complete authentication token.

- use DREVORA unlawfully or fraudulently;

- bypass security or role restrictions;
- upload malicious or unlawful content;
- interfere with or overload the Service;
- copy, resell or reverse engineer the Service except where law permits;
- harass, discriminate against or unlawfully monitor another person;
- use the application while driving.
